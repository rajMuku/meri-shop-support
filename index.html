About Meri Shop

Meri Shop helps users discover and explore shops and services available around them. Users can browse shops, view shop information, explore menus, and use other features available in the app.

Login and Account Help

Unable to log in

Please make sure you are using the correct login details and that your internet connection is working properly.

Account problems

If you are unable to access your account or experience an account-related problem, please contact our support team.

Location and Nearby Shops

Meri Shop may use your device location to help show nearby shops and services. Please make sure Location Services are enabled on your device if you want to use nearby features.

Account Deletion

If you want to delete your Meri Shop account and associated account data, please contact our support team using the email address below and request account deletion.

We will process account deletion requests according to our applicable policies and legal requirements.
